## Design & Content Brief for Claude Code

**Project goal:** Build the English-language author website for **Mikael Viima** as the primary international literary identity of Finnish writer **Manne Mikael Laukkanen** to be deployed to GitHub Pages. Division: Mikael Viima for fiction and poetry; Manne Laukkanen for nonfiction. This site is for Mikael Viima. Manne Laukkanen's nonfiction appears as a secondary credential under his own name, never presented as Mikael Viima's work.

**Primary audience:** English-speaking readers, editors, publishers, agents, translators, reviewers, festival programmers, and literary media.

**Core principle:** This is not a Finnish author website translated into English. It is an **English-market author website** that happens to have an already-published Finnish career behind it.

## Primary identity

Use **MIKAEL VIIMA** as the dominant name everywhere:

- Header

- Social preview images
- Books and poetry pages
- Press material
- Footer

The real/legal name **Manne Mikael Laukkanen** belongs on the About page, press bio, nonfiction metadata, and copyright/legal material where appropriate.

Do not visually treat “Mikael Viima” and “Manne Laukkanen” as two equal brands.

Preferred formulation:

> **Mikael Viima is the literary name of Finnish writer Manne Mikael Laukkanen.**

“Mikael” is the author's actual middle name. “Viima” is a Finnish word associated with a cold, cutting wind. The name therefore already carries the right sonic and conceptual qualities for marketing: short, concise, northern, sharp, slightly hostile, memorable.

Do not over-explain the name on the home page. It can appear as a small detail on the About page.

# 2. The Author Persona

## Persona in one paragraph

Mikael Viima is a Finnish literary writer with a hard-boiled exterior and the analytical interior of a scientist: a novelist and poet with a PhD in psychology, doctoral work connected to statistical serial-criminal profiling, decades of experience with data and complex systems, a former professional musician, with an appetite for subjects people often prefer to soften — mental instability, mortality, damage, manipulation, sex, power, science, aging, art, class, war, memory and self-deception.

He is a writer whose exceptionally multifaceted life and intellectual background makes his work a wider lens to the human condition, dangerous, exact, and difficult to bullshit.


## Persona keywords

- literate
- unsentimental
- psychologically exact
- dryly funny
- observant
- hard-boiled
- intellectually restless
- cosmopolitan
- northern without being folkloric
- experienced rather than youthful
- skeptical
- capable of tenderness without sentimentality
- willing to look at ugly things directly
- comfortable moving between science and art
- serious about language
- allergic to pomposity

## Voice

The site voice should be:

- concise
- adult
- confident without self-coronation
- darkly amused
- concrete
- rhythmically controlled
- written in natural contemporary English
- occasionally aphoristic
- willing to use a short sentence as a punch
- free of academic jargon unless a technical term is genuinely useful

The copy can imply danger, damage and psychological extremity. 
It should not perform “edginess.”
This writer is a PhD criminal profiler who read 20 years of KRP homicide case files, worked live for ongoing serial crime investigations, stood in front and behind the flashy curtains of global power as a military musician in Guard's Band at state events next to Presidents and sees through the performances of being human. Or anything.

### Good voice

> I write about the points where explanations and excuses stop working and only brutality of existence remains.

> Violence is interesting long before the first blow. So is love before the first touch.

> The human animal is very good at producing distracting noise where their actions speak something else, way louder. I write about the latter.

### Wrong voice

> Dr. Laukkanen combines his extensive academic expertise with decades of multidisciplinary experience to create unique narratives at the intersection of psychology, technology and society.

That would be LinkedIn. Do not build LinkedIn.

# 5. Mood and Visual Direction

## Overall mood

Imagine:

- winter daylight rather than blackness
- carbon paper
- bone-white book paper
- cold air
- an empty Helsinki street at 05:30
- a desk under one hard lamp
- a crime file with no fake police props
- an expensive literary journal
- a brutalist gallery catalogue
- a poem printed cleanly enough that it has nowhere to hide

The website should feel **cold, tactile, intelligent and human**.

It should not feel gothic.

It should not feel cyberpunk.

It should not feel like a streaming-series crime poster.

## Design principle

**Restraint creates menace better than decoration.**

Use large type, quiet space, strong alignment and excellent text rendering. Let one unsettling sentence do more work than five visual effects.

# 7. Typography

## Desired feel

Editorial, literary, modern, unpretentious.

The typography should communicate that the author cares about the physical behavior of words.

### Recommended pairing

### Recommended pairing

**Display / literary serif:** `Newsreader`, `Source Serif 4`, or similar
Use for large headings, book titles, essays, About-page prose, and other editorial text.

**UI / metadata / navigation:** `Inter`, `IBM Plex Sans`, or similar
Use for navigation, buttons, captions, metadata, forms, and other functional interface elements.

**Poetry / manuscript text:** `IBM Plex Mono`, `Source Code Pro`, or another refined true monospaced face
Poems should be rendered in monospace by default. Mikael Viima composes poetry in Linux text editors and uses fixed-width spacing, indentation, and line architecture as part of the work itself. The website should preserve that visual structure rather than translating the poems into conventional literary typography.

Preferred overall combination:

* **Newsreader** for large literary display text and prose
* **Inter** for navigation, metadata, and practical UI
* **IBM Plex Mono** for poem bodies, poem excerpts, manuscript material, and selected labels such as `POETRY / MANUSCRIPT / 2026`

For poetry, authored whitespace should be preserved exactly. Do not normalize indentation, collapse multiple spaces, or automatically reformat line structure. Responsive layouts should adapt around the poem rather than casually altering its internal architecture.

The overall contrast should feel intentional:

**The website speaks in serif. The poems speak in monospace.**

If loading external fonts complicates performance or privacy, use high-quality local or system fallbacks, but retain the distinction between proportional editorial typography and true monospaced poetry typography.


## Typography rules

- Do not make all headings uppercase.
- Use uppercase only for small navigational or metadata labels.
- Body line length: approximately 60–75 characters.
- Poetry must preserve intentional lineation and whitespace.
- Never justify body text.
- Avoid exaggerated letter-spacing.
- Avoid overly thin font weights.
- Minimum body text approximately 18 px on desktop, 17 px on mobile.
- Give poetry more vertical breathing room than prose.

Mikael Viima is the literary name of **Manne Mikael Laukkanen**, a Finnish writer with a PhD in psychology and a background spanning criminal profiling research, technology, data and music.

His first crime novel, **Verijälki**, was published by WSOY in 2022. **Rekyyli** followed in 2023. In 2024, Otava published his nonfiction book **Vaaralliset gurut** under his real name.

He now writes poetry and fiction for a wider English-speaking readership.

---

## About section: The useful collision

### Psychology explains less than people hope. Literature can sometimes get closer.

Viima's doctoral work concerned the profiling of serial criminals. He has also spent decades working with data, software and large information systems across industries, in global settings.

The combination has left a mark.

One discipline asks what a person is likely to do.

Another asks what the system is actually doing.

Writing gets to ask the more uncomfortable question:

**What if both descriptions are wrong?**

His fiction and poetry move repeatedly through psychology, desire, love, memory, aging, science, mortality, art, class, power, politics, manipulation and damage.

---

## About section: Why Mikael Viima

“Mikael” is part of his real name.

“Viima” is a Finnish word associated with a cold, cutting wind.

The name began as the identity used for fiction. It now serves as the main literary identity for fiction and poetry in international markets.

---

## About section: Statement

### Why write?

Because some things still seem unsaid.

Or said badly.

Or softened until they no longer resemble the thing itself.

The poems and novels begin there.

# 32. Tone Rules for Future Copy

Every new paragraph should pass these tests.

### Is it concrete?

Prefer:

> The investigator wakes with blood under his fingernails.

Not:

> A gripping psychological journey begins.

### Is it earned?

Prefer:

> He holds a PhD in psychology; his doctoral work concerned serial-criminal profiling.

Not:

> One of Europe's leading psychological minds.

### Does it respect the reader?

Prefer implication over explanation.

Do not tell the reader a passage is “shocking,” “powerful,” “provocative,” or “brutal.” Make it so.

### Does it sound like Mikael Viima?

If the copy becomes polite, inspirational, corporate or wellness-adjacent, rewrite it.
